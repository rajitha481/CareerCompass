# CareerCompass – GenAI Career Planner

**CareerCompass** is a GenAI-powered career planning solution designed to help **women returning to the workforce after a career break** find suitable career opportunities and understand the skills and steps needed to become job-ready.

🔗 **Live Demo:**
https://partyrock.aws/u/Rajitha481/ISBH7RS2i/CareerCompass

---

##  Problem

Women returning to the workforce after a career break often struggle to find suitable job opportunities due to outdated skills, changing industry requirements, and uncertainty about the right career path. They lack personalized guidance to identify their skill gaps and understand the steps needed to become job-ready.

---

##  Solution

CareerCompass provides personalized career guidance by analyzing a user's background, skills, experience, and career goals.

It helps users understand suitable career options, identify skill gaps, and receive a personalized roadmap with actionable steps for career re-entry.

The solution follows a structured career-planning approach:

**Profile → Career Goal → Skill Gap Analysis → Personalized Roadmap → Action Steps**

---

## ✨ Features

*  **Personalized Career Profile**

  * Education
  * Career status
  * Experience
  * Technical skills
  * Career goals

* 🎯 **Career Recommendations**

  * Suggests relevant career paths based on the user's profile and goals.

*  **Skill-Gap Analysis**

  * Identifies skills that need to be developed for the target career.

* 🗺️ **Personalized Career Roadmap**

  * Generates a structured roadmap based on the user's current situation and target career.

* 📚 **Learning Guidance**

  * Recommends areas and skills that the user should focus on.

* ✅ **Actionable Career Steps**

  * Converts the roadmap into practical steps that users can follow.

*  **GenAI-Powered Personalization**

  * Generates recommendations and career guidance dynamically according to the user's inputs.

---

## 🔄 User Journey

CareerCompass follows a structured five-step workflow:

```text
Profile
   ↓
Career Goal
   ↓
Skill Gap Analysis
   ↓
Personalized Roadmap
   ↓
Action Steps
```

### 1. Profile

The user provides information about their education, career status, skills, experience, and background.

### 2. Career Goal

The user identifies the career direction or job role they want to pursue.

### 3. Skill Gap Analysis

The system analyzes the user's current skills against the skills needed for the desired career.

### 4. Personalized Roadmap

GenAI generates a customized career and learning roadmap based on the identified gaps.

### 5. Action Steps

The user receives clear steps to improve their skills and move toward suitable career opportunities.

##  What Makes CareerCompass Different?

CareerCompass focuses on a **structured career-planning workflow** rather than simply providing job listings.

Instead of:

```text
User → Job Listings
```

CareerCompass provides:

```text
User Profile
     ↓
Career Goal
     ↓
Skill Gap
     ↓
Personalized Roadmap
     ↓
Action Steps
     ↓
Career Readiness
```

This makes the platform useful for women who need guidance on **how to return to the workforce**, not just where to search for jobs.
